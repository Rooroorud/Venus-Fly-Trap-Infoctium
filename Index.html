Leaderboard

Strongest

Top 3 Strongest Cultivars

3. Jaws

Jaws is a vigorous Venus flytrap cultivar with distinctive sawtoothed traps, very fast closing speed, and a high catch rate. It is a more common cultivar that is still very strong and is a great all-rounder. It is very good for catching stronger bugs just like B52 and DC XL, but isn't as good though can catch small bugs very well.

2. DC XL

DC XL is known for its huge traps, which are around the same size as B52's however are not as thick or strong as B52's traps. The traps are more high up than B52s which are practically touching the ground. DC XL is still a very strong cultivar and is a great choice if you want to kill more small insects.

1. B52

B52 has one of the thickest walls of any venus fly trap cultivar, has fast closing speed, massive traps, and has an very high catch rate. This makes it the strongest venus fly trap cultivar for catching stronger bugs such as wasps, hornets, and even some beetles. It has very low, practically touching the ground traps. It is a monster for catching strong prey and is the strongest fly trap cultivar in all of existence

Biggest

Overall Leaderboard

1. B52

The B52 produces some of the largest traps in the world, and they do it consistently. Almost all B52 traps are the same size, gigantic, monster sized traps. B52 is the undisputed champion of the biggest venus flytrap cultivars when looking at consistency and overall size.

2. King Henry

King Henry is a very large cultivar that not only produces some of the largest traps in the world, but also produces them consistently. They are great for beginners, with excellent vigour and growth rate, it is highly recommendable.

3. DC XL

DC XL has been known for having massive traps, sometimes even beating B52 in size. However, although it can be that big, they are not as consistent as B52 or King Henry. While DC XL is still a massive cultivar, it doesnt have the consistency that the other cultivars have.

Potential Leaderboard

1. GJ Montecore

The GJ Montecore is one of the biggest venus fly trap cultivars when you look at its growth potential. It can produce some of the largest traps in the world, which can outsize B52 and King Henry. However, this cultivar is not consistent and also requires a lot of care to reach its full potential size.

2. Alien

Alien is another large cultivar with insanely high growth potential. They are known to have extremely inconsistent trap sizes and weird growth patterns, but they have the potential to have traps near the size of the GJ Montecore and can outsize B52. They are very inconsistent, which makes them a very risky cultivar to grow, but when they do grow massive, they are massive.

3. DC XL

DC XL has slightly higher growth potential than B52, though not as consistent, they can grow some massive traps. They have very high vigour and are very easy to grow, which also makes them perfect for beginners. Although they do not have as much growth potential as GJ Montecore or Alien, they are much more consistent than them which actually makes them better most of the time.
